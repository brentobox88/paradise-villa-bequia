Inside, the home is just as inviting. The open-plan kitchen features warm wood cabinetry, a mosaic tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, and a spacious breakfast bar. It's fully equipped for anything from quick snacks to elaborate island feasts.

The expansive balcony spans over 1,200 square feet, offering ample space for hosting everything from intimate dinners to small or medium-sized destination wedding parties. With multiple levels of shaded, arched balconies, there's always a breezy spot to curl up with a book.
